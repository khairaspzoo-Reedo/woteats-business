Group ordering

Everyone orders. You keep the purse.

Turn it on, set the rules, share one link. People pick their own plates. You can edit or cancel before the kitchen starts.

  1. Turn on group ordering.
    From the office account. Name the day and the office.
  2. Set the budget per person.
    They can spend less. A top-up is theirs, not yours, if you allow it.
  3. Choose the kitchens.
    A short list, or the whole town. Diets stay filterable.
  4. Share the link.
    A reminder goes out. Plates are packed with a name on each.
  5. One drop. One invoice.
    Live progress on the order. The bill goes to the invoice email.
